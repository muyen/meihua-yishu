EVENT: Commonwealth Games track cycling at the Sir Chris Hoy Velodrome
HEXAGRAM LABEL: B
PRIMARY: 9 風天小畜 -> CHANGED: 1 乾為天 (Line 4)
MUTUAL: 火澤睽

1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Wind above heaven: Xiao Xu, the Small Accumulating. Something large is being restrained by something small — the judgment's image is "密雲不雨", dense clouds but no rain. Pressure gathers and is not yet released. The body trigram Xun (wood) controls the use trigram Qian (metal) in the reading given here: the subject exerts a check on a much stronger force.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Xun as body: penetrating, gradual, working by insinuation rather than confrontation.
- Qian as use: heaven, unrelenting strength, the thing being held back.
- The fourth line is correct in its place with a proper correspondent, but sits above a yang line — a soft position holding a hard one, structurally the least stable arrangement. Its text: "有孚，血去惕出，无咎" — there is confidence; the blood departs, apprehension goes out; no blame. Injury and fear both drain away.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc is one of accumulated pressure under restraint. The fourth line is where the danger actually passes: blood leaves, alarm leaves, and what remains is trust. This is a release of a threat rather than an achievement of a goal. The mutual hexagram Kui (睽, Opposition) marks internal divergence along the way. The transformation is the most emphatic in the book — into Qian (乾), pure heaven, six unbroken lines. Every restraint is removed at once; the small accumulation is overwhelmed by undiluted force.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
A danger survived, then total dominance by the stronger party. The first movement is escape from harm; the second is the collapse of whatever was doing the restraining. The overall shape is of a check that holds only briefly before the larger power expresses itself completely.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- Heavy clouds that will not rain: everything gathered, nothing yet released.
- Blood leaving the wound and fear leaving the room: two kinds of relief arriving together.
- A small thing tethering a large one: restraint that depends on position rather than strength.
- Six unbroken lines: pure, undivided force with nothing left to obstruct it.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
The surprise is the scale of the ending. Xiao Xu is a hexagram of modest, partial holding, and it converts here into the most complete image of power in the sequence. The warning implied: whatever restraint is operating is temporary, and its removal is not gradual. Do not build on the assumption that the small check persists.
