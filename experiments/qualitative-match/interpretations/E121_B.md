EVENT: Northern hemisphere heat extremes
HEXAGRAM LABEL: B
PRIMARY: 25 天雷无妄 -> CHANGED: 13 天火同人 (Line 3)
MUTUAL: 風山漸

1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Heaven above thunder: Wu Wang, Innocence — action without calculation, the natural order in motion. The judgment is 元亨利貞 with a sharp qualifier: "其匪正有眚", if it is not upright there will be calamity. The body trigram Zhen (wood) controls the use trigram — the subject holds the initiative over the situation.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Zhen as body: sudden, honest movement; the impulse that does not scheme.
- Qian as use: heaven, overwhelming authority, the frame everything happens inside.
- The third line is incorrect in its place though it has a correspondent. Its text: "无妄之災，或繫之牛，行人之得，邑人之災" — the calamity of innocence: someone ties up an ox, a passing traveller takes it, and the townsmen are blamed. Misfortune arrives with no wrongdoing attached to the person who suffers it.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc begins in unforced correctness and is interrupted by a loss that nobody earned. The ox is tethered properly; a stranger walks off with it; the village bears the consequence. This is the purest statement in the book of undeserved cost — the transfer of a gain to one party and a penalty to another with no moral logic between them. The mutual hexagram Jian (漸, Gradual Progress) runs underneath: things advance step by step regardless. The transformation into Tong Ren (同人, Fellowship) — fire under heaven — resolves the isolation: the scattered are gathered into common cause.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
An undeserved loss absorbed, followed by consolidation among those who share the position. The flavour is of somebody paying for something they did not do, and of the affected parties finding each other afterwards. Not catastrophic; genuinely unfair.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- The tethered ox taken by a passer-by: a gain for one that is a loss for another, with no fault involved.
- The townsmen blamed for a stranger's act: consequence landing on the wrong address.
- Thunder rolling beneath an open sky: honest force under an indifferent authority.
- Fellowship in the open field: those who were separately affected finding common ground.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
The distinctive warning of Wu Wang is that correctness does not immunise. The subject here does nothing wrong and still absorbs the cost. The surprise is who benefits: 行人, the traveller — an outside party passing through, not a rival within the frame. Watch for the beneficiary being someone not previously in the picture.
