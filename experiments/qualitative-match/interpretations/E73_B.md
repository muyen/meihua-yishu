PRIMARY: 17 澤雷隨 -> CHANGED: 25 天雷无妄 (Line 6)
MUTUAL: 風山漸

1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Lake above thunder: Sui, Following — but read at the top line, where following has run to its end. The use trigram attacks the body trigram (用克體): the situation exerts cutting pressure on the subject. This is the least favourable of the five elemental relations.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- The body faces a force that structurally dominates it.
- Dui above: the mouth, exchange, the surface on which deals are struck.
- Zhen below: sudden movement, the impulse to act.
- The top line is correct in place but unanswered, and rests on a firm line beneath — soft over hard, the unstable arrangement. Its text: "拘係之，乃從維之，王用亨于西山" — he is seized and bound, then tied fast; the king makes an offering on the western mountain. Someone is not merely followed but restrained, and the episode closes with a formal ceremony.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc ends in binding. Following, which began as voluntary adaptation, terminates in something being tied down and held — the character 拘 is arrest, not agreement. The doubling ("seized and bound, then tied fast") emphasises that the restraint is thorough. The mutual hexagram Jian (漸) says the process was gradual: this did not happen suddenly. The transformation into Wu Wang (无妄, Innocence) is the release valve — the situation resolves into unforced naturalness, the state where things simply proceed as they will. Binding gives way to the removal of artifice.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
Something held fast at the end of a long process of accommodation, formalised by ceremony, then resolving into a state where calculation no longer applies. The dominant flavour is constraint — and the pressure comes from outside, against a subject not equipped to resist it.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- Seized, bound, and tied again: restraint applied twice over to make sure.
- The king's offering on the western mountain: a formal rite closing the episode, the ceremonial seal on a deal.
- Thunder trapped under the still surface of a lake.
- Innocence at the end: the state where nobody is manoeuvring any more.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
The elemental relation is the worst in the set, and the top line's instability compounds it: this is a position that cannot push back. The surprise is the ceremony. The line does not end in punishment or escape — it ends in a ritual on a mountain, a public formalisation of whatever was settled. Watch for a binding arrangement that gets sealed rather than broken.
