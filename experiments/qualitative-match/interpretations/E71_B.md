EVENT: Commonwealth Games netball final
HEXAGRAM LABEL: B
PRIMARY: 31 澤山咸 -> CHANGED: 28 澤風大過 (Line 2)
MUTUAL: 天風姤

1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Lake above mountain: Xian, Influence. The lake resting in the hollow at the mountain's top — two things in contact, each shaped by the other. This is the hexagram of mutual attraction, and its line texts run up the body from the toe to the tongue. The use trigram feeds the body trigram: the situation nourishes the subject.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Dui above: the lake, receptive, pleasurable, the open surface.
- Gen below: the mountain, still, the fixed thing being acted on.
- The second line is correct in place with a proper correspondent. Its text: "咸其腓，凶，居吉" — influence in the calf; ominous; staying put is auspicious. The calf is the muscle that moves before the body has decided to move.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc climbs the body one position at a time. At the second rung the impulse has reached the calf — the part that twitches ahead of intention, the premature movement. The line's verdict is split in a way that is unusually explicit: acting is 凶, staying is 吉. Same position, opposite outcomes, determined entirely by whether one moves. The mutual hexagram Gou (姤, Coming to Meet) marks an unplanned arrival inside the process. The transformation into Da Guo (大過, Great Exceeding) — the ridgepole bending, heavy at the centre and weak at both ends — introduces structural overload.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
An early impulse to move that should be resisted, with stillness explicitly rewarded, resolving into a structure carrying more than it comfortably can. The flavour is of the twitch before the decision, and of the situation afterwards being under more load than its supports were designed for.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- The lake settled in the mountain's crown: contact between two things that cannot move toward each other.
- The calf twitching before the body has decided: motion that precedes intention.
- Two verdicts in one line: go and it is bad, stay and it is good.
- The ridgepole sagging under weight concentrated at its middle.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
The Xian sequence is the clearest bottom-to-top ladder in the book, and the second rung is where the ladder issues its only unambiguous prohibition on acting. The surprise is how completely the verdict depends on the actor's own restraint rather than on external events. Expect the favourable path to be the one that does not initiate.
