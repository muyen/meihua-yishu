EVENT: Second weekend of August at the box office
HEXAGRAM LABEL: A
PRIMARY: 56 火山旅 -> CHANGED: 50 火風鼎 (Line 2)
MUTUAL: 澤風大過

1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Fire above mountain: Lü, The Wanderer. A fire burning on a hillside — it consumes what is there and moves on, never staying. The hexagram of the traveller, the outsider without standing in the place they find themselves. The judgment allows only small success: "小亨". The body trigram drains into the use trigram.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Li above: fire, moving across the surface, attached to nothing.
- Gen below: the mountain, the fixed ground that the fire passes over.
- The second line is correct in place and central, but has no answering line. Its text: "旅即次，懷其資，得童僕貞" — the wanderer comes to lodging, carries their means with them, and obtains a young servant's loyalty. The best position in the hexagram: shelter, resources, and one reliable helper.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc is one of movement without belonging, and the second line is where the traveller is most secure. All three elements are provisional — a lodging rather than a home, portable means rather than holdings, one junior helper rather than a household — but they are sufficient. The mutual hexagram Da Guo (大過, Great Exceeding) marks structural strain at the centre. The transformation into Ding (鼎, the Cauldron) — the vessel of transformation, supremely auspicious — converts the traveller's temporary arrangement into something that cooks raw material into a finished substance.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
A modest, self-sufficient position held by an outsider, adequate rather than commanding, resolving into genuine transformation. The flavour is of travelling light and being fine, and of a small provisional setup turning into something that produces a real change of state.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- Fire moving across a hillside: presence without attachment.
- Lodging for the night with one's goods still on one's person.
- A single young servant's loyalty: modest support, reliably given.
- The cauldron over the flame: ingredients becoming a different thing entirely.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
The second line is the one comfortable seat in an uncomfortable hexagram, and it has no correspondent — the security is self-supplied, not granted. The surprise is the transformation into Ding, the hexagram of supreme auspiciousness: the wanderer's small provisional arrangement resolves into the most productive figure available. Expect something modest to convert into something substantial.
