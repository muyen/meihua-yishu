EVENT: NL West standings on 10 August
HEXAGRAM LABEL: A
PRIMARY: 12 天地否 -> CHANGED: 20 風地觀 (Line 4)
MUTUAL: 風山漸

1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Heaven above earth: Pi, Standstill. The two primal trigrams in their natural positions and therefore moving apart — heaven rising, earth sinking, no exchange between them. The judgment: "天地不交", heaven and earth do not communicate. The body trigram drains into the use trigram: the subject expends itself into the situation.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Qian above: authority, remote and rising away.
- Kun below: the mass, settling downward.
- The fourth line is incorrect in place but has a correspondent, and sits over a yielding line — firm above soft, the most orderly of the vertical relations. Its text: "有命无咎，疇離祉" — there is a mandate; no blame; the companions share in the blessing. In the middle of total blockage, an authorisation arrives and the benefit is distributed to a group.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc is one of stalled communication. Nothing passes between the levels. At the fourth line something breaks the deadlock — but it comes as 命, a mandate handed down, not as an achievement earned from below. What follows is notable: the benefit does not stay with the recipient. 疇 means the field-companions, the peers; they all partake. The mutual hexagram Jian (漸) again indicates gradual movement inside the stalemate. The transformation into Guan (觀, Contemplation) — wind over earth — shifts the register entirely: from blockage to observation, the tower from which one surveys.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
A blockage broken from above by authorisation, with the benefit spread across a group rather than captured by one party, resolving into a period of watching. The flavour is of a decision handed down that unblocks things for several parties at once, followed by everyone stepping back to look at what they now have.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- Heaven and earth drawing apart: two halves of a system that have stopped speaking.
- A mandate arriving from above: permission, not accomplishment.
- Companions sharing the blessing: a benefit that distributes rather than concentrates.
- Wind moving over the earth: the surveying gaze that passes across everything.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
Pi is one of the two great obstruction hexagrams, yet the fourth line is straightforwardly favourable — the relief is real. The warning sits in its source: it is granted, not won, so it can equally be withdrawn. The surprise is the sharing. Whatever advantage appears here does not accrue to one holder; expect several parties to benefit from the same event.
