EVENT: US July employment report
HEXAGRAM LABEL: A
PRIMARY: 15 地山謙 -> CHANGED: 36 地火明夷 (Line 1)
MUTUAL: 雷水解

1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Earth above mountain: Qian, Modesty. A mountain that sits below the ground — the one hexagram in the book where every single line is favourable, without exception. The judgment: "君子有終", the noble person carries things to completion. Body and use are in 比和, harmonious like natures.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Kun above: earth, level and broad.
- Gen below: the mountain, enormous, and positioned underneath.
- The first line is incorrect in place and unanswered — the weakest structural position in the figure. Its text: "謙謙君子，用涉大川，吉" — the doubly modest noble person; with this one may cross the great water; auspicious. The modesty is doubled for emphasis, and it licenses the largest undertaking in the book's vocabulary.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc begins from the lowest and least supported position, and the text responds by doubling down on lowness rather than compensating for it. 謙謙 — modest upon modest — is what makes the great crossing possible. The mutual hexagram Jie (解, Deliverance) indicates a knot loosening within the process. The transformation into Ming Yi (明夷, Darkening of the Light) shifts the register: the sun goes below the earth, brightness continues but must be concealed. Modesty becomes enforced obscurity — the same lowness, no longer chosen.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
A position of no advantage that succeeds precisely by not claiming any, resolving into a phase where visibility is lost. The flavour is of something starting from the bottom and being rewarded for staying there, then finding the darkness closing in — the low position remaining, the choice about it removed.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- A mountain buried under flat ground: something enormous that does not show above the surface.
- Modesty doubled: lowness insisted upon, not merely accepted.
- Crossing the great water from the weakest position on the board.
- The sun sinking below the horizon: light that continues underground.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
Qian is the exception in the book — no line carries a warning — so the absence of caution here is a property of the hexagram rather than a judgment about the situation. The surprise is the destination: the only unambiguously favourable hexagram transforms into one of the darkest. What begins as chosen humility ends as imposed obscurity.
