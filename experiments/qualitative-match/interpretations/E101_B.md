EVENT: Second weekend of August at the box office
HEXAGRAM LABEL: B
PRIMARY: 62 雷山小過 -> CHANGED: 16 雷地豫 (Line 3)
MUTUAL: 澤風大過

1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Thunder above mountain: Xiao Guo, Small Exceeding. Four yielding lines around two firm ones at the centre — the shape of a bird in flight. The judgment is explicit about scale: "可小事，不可大事", small matters yes, great matters no, and the flying bird's message says it is better to stay low. The body trigram controls the use trigram.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Zhen above: thunder, movement, the impulse to rise.
- Gen below: the mountain, stillness, the ground that should be kept close.
- The third line is correct in place with a correspondent, and rests over a yielding line — the orderly firm-over-soft relation. Its text: "弗過防之，從或戕之，凶" — if one does not go beyond in guarding against it, someone following may strike him down; ominous. The one line in this hexagram where insufficient caution is the fault.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc is about staying within a modest compass. At the third line the specific danger is named: failing to over-guard. In a hexagram that warns everywhere else about exceeding, this line inverts the counsel — here the excess should be applied to precaution, and the failure to apply it invites an attack from behind. 從 is the one who comes after, the follower. The mutual hexagram Da Guo (大過, Great Exceeding) sits at the centre, the ridgepole bending: strain concentrated where it is least visible. The transformation into Yu (豫, Enthusiasm) — thunder bursting out of the earth — releases everything into movement and momentum.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
A position that fails to guard sufficiently and is struck from behind, resolving into a surge of collective movement. The flavour is of complacency punished by someone coming from the rear, and of the aftermath being noisy and energetic rather than quiet.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- The flying bird whose call says stay low.
- Failing to over-guard: the precaution that was reasonable and not sufficient.
- The follower who strikes: danger arriving from behind, from someone already in the picture.
- Thunder coming out of the earth: energy released all at once after being held.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
This line's 凶 is unambiguous and its cause is precisely specified — not too much action but too little defence. The surprise is the inversion: in the hexagram of Small Exceeding, the fault is failing to exceed. The threat comes from a follower, not a leader; watch the position behind rather than the one above.
