HEXAGRAM LABEL: A
PRIMARY: 22 山火賁 -> CHANGED: 27 山雷頤 (Line 3)
MUTUAL: 雷水解

1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Mountain above fire: Bi, Grace. Fire at the foot of the mountain illuminating it from below — the hexagram of ornament, of form applied to substance. The judgment is characteristically limited: "亨，小利有攸往", success, and in small matters it is favourable to go somewhere. Adornment does not carry large undertakings. The use trigram feeds the body trigram.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Gen above: the mountain, the substance being decorated.
- Li below: fire, the light that makes the form visible.
- The third line is correct in place but has no answering line, and rests over a yielding line. Its text: "賁如濡如，永貞吉" — adorned and glossy; lasting correctness is auspicious. 濡 is a wet sheen — the ornament has been brought to a high finish.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc concerns surface and what lies under it. At the third line the finish is at its most lustrous — the two characters 賁如濡如 describe decoration carried to a gleam. And precisely there the text inserts 永貞: it is not enough to be correct now, the correctness has to be sustained, because a high polish is exactly the condition that does not last on its own. The mutual hexagram Jie (解, Deliverance) marks a knot loosening at the centre. The transformation into Yi (頤, Nourishment) — the open jaws — shifts attention from surface to intake: from how a thing looks to what it is fed on.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
A high finish achieved, favourable only on condition of being sustained, resolving into a question about substance. The flavour is of something looking very good and of the reading immediately asking whether it can be kept up — the movement is from appearance toward what actually feeds it.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- Fire at the foot of the mountain: light thrown upward on a face, illuminating without warming.
- A wet gloss on a decorated surface: finish at its most impressive and least durable.
- Lasting correctness: the requirement that the state be maintained, not merely reached.
- The open jaws: the shift from how something appears to what it consumes.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
Bi is the hexagram most sceptical about its own subject — ornament is granted only small scope from the outset. The third line has no correspondent, so the polish is self-maintained. The surprise is the transformation into Nourishment: the question moves from the surface to the substance underneath it, which is exactly the question Grace tends to avoid.
