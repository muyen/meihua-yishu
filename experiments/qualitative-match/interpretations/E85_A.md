1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Wind above fire: Jia Ren, The Family. Fire produces wind, and the wind carries the fire's influence outward — the hexagram of the ordered household, where roles are correctly assigned and the order propagates from inside out. The judgment: "利女貞", favourable for the correctness of the woman, the interior principle. The use trigram feeds the body trigram: the situation nourishes the subject.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Xun as body: wind, penetration, the influence that spreads.
- Li as use: fire, the source of light and heat at the centre.
- The top line is incorrect in place and unanswered. Its text: "有孚威如，終吉" — there is confidence, and dignity of bearing; in the end, auspicious. Two qualities named together: inner trustworthiness and outward authority. 威如 is the composure that commands without shouting.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc runs from an ordered interior to an authority that others accept. At the top the position has both sincerity and gravity, and the outcome is placed explicitly at the end — 終吉, auspicious eventually, not immediately. The mutual hexagram Wei Ji (未濟, Before Completion) sits at the centre: everything out of place, the crossing not finished. The transformation into Ji Ji (既濟, After Completion) is the exact resolution of that — every line moving into its correct position, the crossing accomplished. The situation moves from incompleteness to completion.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
Authority earned through consistency rather than assertion, arriving late and settling everything into place. The flavour is of a slow accumulation of standing, of respect that is granted rather than demanded, and of a final configuration in which every element is where it belongs.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- Wind carrying the warmth of a fire outward: interior order propagating to the outside.
- Dignity of bearing: presence that does not need to raise its voice.
- Ji Ji at the end: the small fox crossing the river with every foot on the right stone.
- The household with everyone in their proper role.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
The good fortune is explicitly deferred — 終吉 means the outcome is at the end, and the intermediate stretch is not described as comfortable. The top line has no correspondent, so the authority is not conferred by anyone; it accrues. The surprise is the completeness of the resolution: Wei Ji at the centre and Ji Ji at the end means the situation ends in the most fully ordered figure in the book.
