PRIMARY: 61 風澤中孚 -> CHANGED: 42 風雷益 (Line 2)
MUTUAL: 山雷頤

1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Wind above lake: Zhong Fu, Inner Truth. Two yielding lines at the centre between firm lines — the empty heart, sincerity without obstruction. The judgment: even pigs and fish are moved; it is favourable to cross the great water. The use trigram attacks the body trigram: the situation cuts against the subject.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Xun above: wind, the influence that carries.
- Dui below: the lake, open, resonant.
- The second line is central but incorrect in place and unanswered. Its text is the most lyrical in the book: "鳴鶴在陰，其子和之，我有好爵，吾與爾靡之" — a crane calls in the shade and its young answers; I have a good goblet and I will share it with you. Resonance across distance, and an offer of something valued.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc concerns influence that works without contact. The crane is in shadow — unseen — and the answering call comes anyway; the connection is by sound alone, between parties who cannot see each other. The second half turns to willing sharing: not a transaction but an offer. The mutual hexagram Yi (頤, Nourishment) sits at the centre, the open jaws. The transformation into Yi (益, Increase) — wind above thunder, the hexagram in which the upper is decreased to augment the lower — makes the sharing structural: benefit deliberately moved downward.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
Connection established without visibility, followed by voluntary sharing, resolving into a transfer of benefit downward. The flavour is warm and uncoerced — the rare register in this book where the mechanism is resonance rather than force, and where what happens next is that someone gives something away on purpose.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- A crane calling from the shade and its young answering: connection by sound between parties who cannot see one another.
- A good goblet offered and shared: value handed over without a bargain.
- The empty centre: receptivity that makes resonance possible.
- Increase: the upper diminished so the lower gains.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
The elemental relation is the cutting one and the position has no correspondent, which sits oddly against the warmth of the text — the pressure is real even though the imagery is generous. The surprise is how little is transactional here: the defining action of this reading is giving something away, and the transformation confirms it.
