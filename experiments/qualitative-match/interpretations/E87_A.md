HEXAGRAM LABEL: A
PRIMARY: 6 天水訟 -> CHANGED: 59 風水渙 (Line 4)
MUTUAL: 風火家人

1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Heaven above water: Song, Conflict. Heaven rises, water sinks — two forces moving apart, which is the structure of a dispute. The judgment is discouraging about pursuing it: "終凶", carried to the end it is ominous, and "不利涉大川", not favourable to cross the great water. The use trigram feeds the body trigram: the situation nourishes the subject.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Qian above: authority, the party that cannot be argued down.
- Kan below: water, danger, the depth from which the grievance rises.
- The fourth line is incorrect in place, has a correspondent, and rests over a yielding line — the orderly firm-over-soft relation. Its text: "不克訟，復自命，渝安貞，吉" — one cannot win the dispute; one returns and accepts one's mandate; changing course, one rests in correctness; auspicious.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc is a contest that cannot be won and the decision to stop contesting it. Every element of the fourth line is a reversal: 不克 (cannot prevail), 復 (return), 渝 (change), 安 (settle). The favourable outcome is attached entirely to the withdrawal, not to any concession from the other side. The mutual hexagram Jia Ren (家人, The Family) sits at the centre — the ordered interior, roles properly assigned. The transformation into Huan (渙, Dispersion) breaks the whole configuration up: wind over water, the rigid surface scattering.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
A dispute abandoned rather than resolved, with the abandonment itself producing the good outcome, ending in dissolution of the terms that made it a dispute. The flavour is of someone climbing down and being better for it — and of the whole contested structure dispersing afterwards, so that what was fought over stops being a fixed object.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- Heaven and water moving in opposite directions: the geometry of an argument.
- Returning to accept one's own mandate: standing down and taking the assigned portion.
- The household in order at the centre: internal roles that were never actually in dispute.
- Wind breaking up the water's surface: the object of contention dissolving.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
Song's overall counsel is that winning is not the goal and persisting is ominous — this is the hexagram that recommends settlement. The fourth line is where the recommendation is taken. The surprise is that 吉 attaches to the party who gives way: the favourable reading here belongs to whoever stops pressing, not whoever prevails.
