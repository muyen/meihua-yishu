1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Heaven above thunder: Wu Wang, Innocence. Movement that proceeds without calculation, under an authority that does not negotiate. The judgment is 元亨利貞 with the sharp rider that if one is not upright there will be calamity. The use trigram attacks the body trigram: the situation cuts against the subject.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Qian above: heaven, overwhelming and impersonal.
- Zhen below: thunder, the honest impulse that acts without scheming.
- The fifth line holds the sovereign position, is correct in place, and has a proper correspondent. Its text: "无妄之疾，勿藥有喜" — the illness of innocence; do not medicate and there will be joy. An affliction that arises without fault, and a prohibition on treating it.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc concerns trouble that arrives without being caused. At the ruling position an illness appears — 无妄之疾, sickness belonging to the innocent state, not earned and not deserved. The counsel is counterintuitive and absolute: do not medicate. The affliction resolves on its own, and intervention is what would convert it into a real problem. The mutual hexagram Jian (漸, Gradual Progress) indicates the underlying movement is incremental. The transformation into Shi He (噬嗑, Biting Through) — jaws closing on an obstruction, the hexagram of legal proceedings — supplies the second phase: after the endurance, a decisive removal of what is stuck.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
An undeserved difficulty that resolves by being left alone, followed by decisive intervention against something genuinely lodged. The flavour is two-phase: first restraint against the instinct to fix, then sharp action when the real obstruction becomes identifiable.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- An illness that heals without medicine: the affliction where treatment is the mistake.
- Thunder rolling under an indifferent sky.
- Jaws closing on gristle: the decisive bite that removes what is caught.
- Lightning in a courtroom: judgment rendered in a flash.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
The elemental relation is the cutting one, so the pressure on the position is real even though the line is favourable. The surprise is the prohibition: in the ruling position, where action is expected, the text forbids it. The timing distinction is the whole content — under-react early, act decisively late, and reversing the order is the failure mode.
