EVENT: UEFA Champions League third qualifying round, first legs
HEXAGRAM LABEL: B
PRIMARY: 26 山天大畜 -> CHANGED: 18 山風蠱 (Line 1)
MUTUAL: 雷澤歸妹

1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Mountain above heaven: Da Xu, the Great Accumulating. Heaven held within the mountain — an enormous force contained by something still. The judgment favours firmness and, notably, "不家食吉", it is auspicious not to eat at home: the accumulated strength is meant to be spent outside. The body trigram drains into the use trigram: the subject expends itself.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Gen as body: the mountain, stillness, the container.
- Qian as use: heaven, creative force, what is being stored.
- The first line is correct in place with a proper correspondent. Its text: "有厲，利已" — there is danger; it is favourable to stop. This is one of the shortest and least ambiguous lines in the book: the situation is risky and the correct response is to halt.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc opens with a check. At the very bottom the force wants to move and the text says do not. The whole hexagram is about accumulation through restraint, and the first line establishes that restraint is imposed early, before anything is committed. The mutual hexagram Gui Mei (歸妹, The Marrying Maiden) is the least comfortable image in the set — a subordinate entry into an arrangement not on one's own terms. The transformation into Gu (蠱, Work on the Decayed) — the bowl with worms in it — names the real task: something has rotted through neglect and must be repaired. Accumulation gives way to remediation.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
Strength held back at the start, resolving into repair work on something long neglected. The flavour is of power that does not get spent as intended because attention is diverted to fixing an inherited mess. Not a defeat; a redirection into unglamorous maintenance.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- Heaven inside the mountain: vast energy in a container that does not move.
- The order to halt at the first step: a stop given before any momentum exists.
- The marrying maiden entering on someone else's terms.
- A vessel gone rotten: decay that accumulated quietly and now demands the work.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
The warning is unusually direct — 有厲, there is danger, at the position where danger is least expected. The surprise is Gu: Great Accumulation is a hexagram of stored potential, and it transforms into the hexagram of cleaning up predecessors' damage. Whatever was being saved up gets spent on repair rather than on the intended purpose.
