1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Fire above earth: Jin, Progress. The sun rising over open ground — the hexagram of advancement, of light spreading and rank increasing. The judgment describes a prince receiving horses in great numbers and being received three times in a single day. The body trigram drains into the use trigram: the subject expends itself.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Li above: fire, the rising sun, visibility.
- Kun below: earth, the flat ground the light spreads across.
- The third line is incorrect in place but has a correspondent. Its text: "眾允，悔亡" — the crowd assents; regret disappears. Four characters. The advancement is validated not by authority above but by the agreement of the many.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc is one of rising visibility, and at the third line the rise is ratified from below. 眾允 is collective consent — the position is incorrect in itself, and what makes it work is that everyone concurs. The regret that would otherwise attach dissolves for that reason alone. The mutual hexagram Jian (蹇, Obstruction) — water above mountain, the hardest travelling in the book — sits at the centre: the path is difficult even while the light rises. The transformation into Lü (旅, The Wanderer) — fire on the mountain, a fire that burns through and moves on — converts the advancement into transience: the traveller with no fixed place.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
An advance validated by collective agreement rather than by authority, over difficult ground, resolving into rootlessness. The flavour is of consensus carrying something that could not stand on its own merits, and of the resulting position turning out to be temporary rather than established.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- The sun coming up over flat ground: light spreading where there is nothing to block it.
- The crowd's assent: validation from numbers rather than from rank.
- Obstruction at the centre: hard going concealed inside a rising story.
- Fire on the mountain: a blaze that consumes its ground and moves on.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
The third position is structurally weak here, and the line's entire remedy is other people's agreement — remove the consensus and nothing supports the position. The surprise is the destination: Progress resolving into The Wanderer, one of the more unsettled figures in the book. Expect an advance that does not consolidate into a permanent place.
