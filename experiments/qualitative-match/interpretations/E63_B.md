HEXAGRAM LABEL: B
PRIMARY: 41 山澤損 -> CHANGED: 38 火澤睽 (Line 4)
MUTUAL: 地雷復

1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Mountain above lake: Sun, Decrease. Something is deliberately taken from below to add above — the lake is deepened so the mountain may stand higher. The judgment allows that even a very modest offering suffices: "二簋可用享", two bowls are enough for the sacrifice. The use trigram Dui (metal) generates the body trigram Gen (earth): the situation feeds the subject.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Gen as body: stillness, the mountain, the position that holds and accumulates.
- Dui as use: the lake, pleasure, exchange, what is given away.
- The fourth line is correct in its place and has a proper correspondent below. Its text: "損其疾，使遄有喜，无咎" — decrease the illness; make haste and there will be joy; no blame. The thing being reduced is an affliction, and speed is explicitly rewarded.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc begins with voluntary reduction — something is given up, and the giving up is the correct move rather than a loss. The fourth line names the object: an illness, a chronic drag that has been carried too long. Acting quickly removes it. The mutual hexagram Fu (復, Return) sits inside: a turning point, the first stirring of light after the solstice. The transformation into Kui (睽, Opposition) is the sting in the tail — fire above lake, two elements that move apart from each other. What was consolidated by decrease then divides.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
Relief obtained by giving something up promptly, followed by divergence. The dominant flavour is of a burden shed and health restored, but the closing register is estrangement — parties that were aligned during the sacrifice discover they are looking in different directions.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- Two bowls of offering: the small, plain gesture that is nevertheless sufficient.
- Hollowing the lake to raise the mountain: one side deliberately depleted so another may rise.
- An illness treated in time: haste rewarded, delay punished.
- Fire rising, lake sinking: two things in the same frame moving irreversibly apart.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
The warning is in the word 遄 — haste. This is one of the few line texts that makes speed itself the condition of the good outcome; the same act performed late does not carry the same result. The surprise is the ending: Decrease resolves not into abundance but into Opposition. Whatever is fixed here does not hold the parties together afterward.
