1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Water above lake: Jie, Limitation. The bounded basin and what flows into it — the joint in the bamboo, the notch that measures. The judgment warns that limitation itself can be carried too far: bitter limitation cannot be persisted in. The body trigram drains into the use trigram: the subject expends itself.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Kan above: water, flow and danger.
- Dui below: the lake, the container with a rim.
- The fourth line is correct in place with a proper correspondent — one of the sounder positions in the figure. Its text: "安節，亨" — limitation accepted with ease; success. Four characters, no qualification, no warning. The restraint is neither resented nor strained.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc concerns keeping to a measure. At the fourth line the measure has stopped being an imposition — 安 is the settled, comfortable acceptance of a limit as simply the shape of things. This is the only line in the hexagram where the limitation costs nothing. The mutual hexagram Yi (頤, Nourishment) sits at the centre: the open jaws, the question of intake. The transformation into Dui (兌) doubled — lake above lake — removes the containing danger entirely and leaves pure joy and open exchange: two bodies of water communicating freely.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
A limit accepted without friction, producing straightforward success, resolving into open and pleasurable exchange. The flavour is of everything staying within its proper bounds and nobody minding, followed by an unusually unguarded and communicative close.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- The bamboo joint: the natural stop that makes the stalk strong rather than shorter.
- A limit worn comfortably: restraint that has stopped registering as restraint.
- Open jaws at the centre: attention on what is taken in.
- Two lakes joined: water communicating across a boundary that no longer holds it apart.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
This is one of the least hedged lines in the batch — no danger, no caution, no deferral. Given that Jie is a hexagram built around the risk of over-restriction, the fourth line is the position where the balance is actually found. The surprise is the transformation: Limitation resolving into doubled Lake, the figure of unrestrained openness. The constraint that worked so well does not persist.
