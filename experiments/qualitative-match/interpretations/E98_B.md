EVENT: Hawaii primary election
HEXAGRAM LABEL: B
PRIMARY: 10 天澤履 -> CHANGED: 38 火澤睽 (Line 5)
MUTUAL: 風火家人

1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Heaven above lake: Lü, Treading. The hexagram of walking on dangerous ground — its image is "履虎尾，不咥人", treading on the tiger's tail without being bitten. Correct conduct in proximity to something that can destroy you. Body and use are in 比和, harmony of like natures: no elemental conflict, and no elemental help either.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Qian above: heaven, the tiger, unrelenting authority.
- Dui below: the lake, pleasure, the light step.
- The fifth line holds the sovereign position, is correct in place, but has no answering line. Its text: "夬履，貞厲" — decisive treading; persistence brings danger. 夬 is the character for resolute breakthrough. The line describes someone stepping with total decisiveness, and warns that exactly this quality is where the danger lies.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc is one of careful movement near a threat. At the ruling position the movement stops being careful and becomes decisive — a change of manner, not of direction. The text does not say the decisiveness fails; it says persisting in it is dangerous. The mutual hexagram Jia Ren (家人, The Family) sits at the centre: internal order, roles properly assigned, the household. The transformation into Kui (睽, Opposition) — fire above lake, two elements moving apart — is the reversal of exactly that: the ordered household becomes estrangement, parties looking in different directions.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
A decisive act taken from a position of authority, carrying a warning attached to its own decisiveness, resolving into division. The flavour is of someone stepping firmly and correctly, and of that firmness being the thing that separates them from those around them.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- Treading on the tiger's tail: proximity to a power that could end you, survived by manner alone.
- The resolute step: no hesitation, and the warning that attaches to none.
- The ordered household at the centre: everyone in their proper role.
- Fire and lake drawing apart: two things in one frame that will not stay together.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
The warning is unusual in being aimed at a virtue: decisiveness is normally rewarded in the sovereign position, and here it is flagged 貞厲. The fifth line's lack of a correspondent means the decision is taken without support from below. The surprise is the movement from Family to Opposition — the situation begins with everything in its right place and ends with the parties turned away from each other.
