EVENT: DEF CON 2026
HEXAGRAM LABEL: B
PRIMARY: 38 火澤睽 -> CHANGED: 54 雷澤歸妹 (Line 6)
MUTUAL: 水火既濟

1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Fire above lake: Kui, Opposition. Fire rises, the lake sinks — two elements in the same frame moving irreversibly apart. The judgment is narrow: "小事吉", in small matters, auspicious. Only limited undertakings succeed here. The use trigram attacks the body trigram (用克體): the situation cuts against the subject.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Li above: fire, sight, brightness — and, in this hexagram, suspicion, because what is seen is misread.
- Dui below: the lake, moving the other way.
- The top line is incorrect in place, has a correspondent, and sits over a yielding line. Its text is the most cinematic in the book: "睽孤，見豕負塗，載鬼一車，先張之弧，後說之弧，匪寇婚媾，往遇雨則吉" — isolated in opposition, one sees a pig covered in mud and a cart full of ghosts; first one draws the bow, then one lays it down; they are not bandits but suitors; going on, one meets rain, and it is auspicious.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc ends in a total misreading that corrects itself. The figure at the top is alone and sees monsters — a filthy pig, a wagon of demons — and reaches for a weapon. Then the bow is lowered: the apparition resolves into a marriage party. What looked like an attack was an approach. The mutual hexagram Ji Ji (既濟, After Completion) sits at the centre — everything already in its place. The transformation into Gui Mei (歸妹, The Marrying Maiden) confirms the correction: this genuinely was a marriage, entered on terms not fully one's own. The closing image is rain, which in this book is release of accumulated tension.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
An alarming appearance that turns out to be its opposite, ending well after a period of hostility based on error. The flavour is of a threat that dissolves on closer inspection, and of the observer's isolation being the actual source of the distortion.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- A pig caked in mud and a cart of ghosts: grotesque apparitions seen from a distance.
- The bow drawn and then lowered: aggression initiated and withdrawn without being fired.
- Bandits who are suitors: the same approach read two opposite ways.
- Rain at the end: tension discharged, the air cleared.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
The elemental relation is cutting and the position is isolated, so the pressure is real — but the entire content of the line is that the perceived threat is a misperception. The surprise is complete inversion: what appears most hostile in this reading is the thing that turns out to be an alliance. Expect an initial reading of events to be dramatically wrong in a favourable direction.
