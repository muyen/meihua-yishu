EVENT: Guam primary election
HEXAGRAM LABEL: A
PRIMARY: 45 澤地萃 -> CHANGED: 17 澤雷隨 (Line 1)
MUTUAL: 風山漸

1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Lake above earth: Cui, Gathering Together. Water collecting on the ground, people assembling — the hexagram of congregation. The judgment mentions the king approaching the temple and that it is favourable to see the great person; a large offering is called for. The use trigram feeds the body trigram: the situation nourishes the subject.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Dui above: the lake, the pooled mass, the shared surface.
- Kun below: earth, the ground everyone stands on.
- The first line is incorrect in place but has a correspondent. Its text is one of the longest in the book: "有孚不終，乃亂乃萃，若號，一握為笑，勿恤，往无咎" — there is confidence but it does not last; there is confusion and then gathering; if you cry out, in one handclasp it turns to laughter; do not worry; going forward, no blame.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc opens with an assembly that does not hold together at first. Trust is present and then lapses; the crowd falls into disorder before it coheres. The pivot is small and physical — 一握, a single grasp of the hand — and it converts distress into laughter. The emotional swing is complete and it happens in one gesture. The mutual hexagram Jian (漸) says the underlying movement is gradual. The transformation into Sui (隨, Following) resolves the confusion into adaptation: people fall in behind something and go along with it.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
A gathering that begins badly, wobbles, and is set right by one small direct contact, resolving into people following along. The flavour is of initial disorder that looks worse than it is, corrected by a gesture rather than a policy, ending in easy alignment.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- Water pooling on flat ground: things collecting because of shape, not because of effort.
- Confidence that does not last: an initial agreement that comes apart.
- One handclasp turning crying into laughter: the smallest possible intervention with the largest emotional swing.
- Falling in behind: the crowd finding something to follow.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
The line explicitly says 勿恤 — do not worry — which is the book's way of flagging that the alarming phase is not the real content. The surprise is the scale mismatch: a serious breakdown of trust is repaired by a single handshake. Expect an early wobble that resolves far more easily than its first appearance suggests.
