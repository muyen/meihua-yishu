EVENT: Major earthquake window
HEXAGRAM LABEL: B
PRIMARY: 27 山雷頤 -> CHANGED: 23 山地剝 (Line 1)
MUTUAL: 坤為地

1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Mountain above thunder: Yi, the Corners of the Mouth. Two firm lines enclosing four yielding ones — the shape of open jaws, the hexagram of nourishment. Its standing question is what a thing feeds on. The use trigram attacks the body trigram: the situation cuts against the subject.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Gen above: the still upper jaw.
- Zhen below: the moving lower jaw.
- The first line is correct in place with a correspondent, but carries a yielding line above it. Its text: "舍爾靈龜，觀我朵頤，凶" — you abandon your own magic tortoise and watch my moving jaws; ominous. The tortoise is the ancient divining shell, the self-sufficient source of guidance.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc concerns provision, and the first line describes the foundational error: setting aside one's own resource in order to watch someone else eat. The 靈龜 is not merely valuable but specifically the instrument by which one knows things for oneself; abandoning it means giving up independent judgment. What replaces it is envy — watching another's jaws work. The verdict is flatly 凶. The mutual hexagram is Kun (坤) doubled, pure yielding through the middle. The transformation into Bo (剝, Splitting Apart) confirms the direction: the staged stripping away, support removed rung by rung.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
Self-sufficiency given up in favour of watching someone else, judged ominous, resolving into progressive loss. The flavour is of a position that had what it needed and looked elsewhere, and of the consequences arriving as steady subtraction rather than as a single blow.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- The magic tortoise set aside: the means of knowing for oneself, discarded.
- Watching another's jaws move: envy in its most physical form.
- Open jaws: the question of what is taken in and from where.
- The bed dismantled from the legs upward: methodical removal of support.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
The elemental relation is the cutting one and the verdict is unqualified 凶 — one of the few lines in this batch with no mitigating clause. The surprise is that the line has a proper correspondent and is correctly placed, which normally indicates a sound position: the fault here is entirely a matter of choice, not of structure. Expect a self-inflicted loss rather than an imposed one.
