1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Water above fire: Ji Ji, After Completion. The only hexagram in which every line occupies its correct place — water above heating fire below, the pot on the stove, everything working. Its judgment carries the warning that belongs to perfection: "初吉終亂", auspicious at the beginning, disordered at the end. The use trigram attacks the body trigram: the situation cuts against the subject.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Kan above: water, correctly placed and inherently dangerous.
- Li below: fire, correctly placed and burning down its fuel.
- The top line is correct in place with a correspondent, but rests on a firm line — the unstable soft-over-hard arrangement. Its text: "濡其首，厲" — he gets his head wet; danger. In the crossing that gives this hexagram its name, wetting the tail is a stumble; wetting the head is going under.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc is completion followed by the unravelling that completion invites. Everything has been done correctly, and the top line is where correctness stops protecting anyone: the crossing that succeeded now submerges the crosser head-first. The mutual hexagram Wei Ji (未濟, Before Completion) — the exact inverse, every line wrong — sits at the centre, so disorder is already resident inside the perfect figure. The transformation into Jia Ren (家人, The Family) is the recovery: the ordered household, roles reassigned, interior structure restored after the immersion.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
A completed thing coming apart at its final step, with the danger arriving after the success rather than before it, resolving into a re-establishment of internal order. The flavour is of the accident that happens after the finish line, and of the repair being organisational rather than heroic.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- Every line in its right place: the figure of things fully working.
- The head going under the water: not a stumble at the edge but a submersion.
- Disorder living inside the completed figure: the inverse hexagram at the centre.
- The household reordered: roles reassigned after the water recedes.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
Ji Ji's own judgment says the trouble comes at the end, and this is that end. The elemental relation is the cutting one, so the pressure is external and real. The surprise is that the most perfectly ordered configuration in the book contains its inverse at its core — the failure is not imported, it was always inside. Expect a problem to emerge after something has apparently been completed.
