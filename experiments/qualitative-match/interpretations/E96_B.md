EVENT: Washington top-two primary
HEXAGRAM LABEL: B
PRIMARY: 47 澤水困 -> CHANGED: 45 澤地萃 (Line 2)
MUTUAL: 風火家人

1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Lake above water: Kun, Oppression. The lake has drained away below and the water sits beneath it — the image is of a marsh with no water in it, exhaustion of resources. The judgment is stark and then unexpectedly firm: "有言不信", words are not believed. The body trigram drains into the use trigram: the subject expends itself.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Dui above: the lake, empty, the surface that should hold water.
- Kan below: water, drained away to the depths where it cannot be reached.
- The second line is central but incorrect in place and unanswered. Its text: "困于酒食，朱紱方來，利用亨祀，征凶，无咎" — oppressed amid wine and food; the vermilion sash is just arriving; it is favourable to make offerings; to march out is ominous; no blame.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc is one of constraint, and the second line's constraint is a peculiar one: the oppression happens in the middle of plenty. There is wine and there is food — the difficulty is not scarcity. The vermilion sash, the insignia of office, is described as 方來, just now arriving: recognition is on its way but has not landed. The counsel is to make offerings and not to march. The mutual hexagram Jia Ren (家人) holds the ordered interior at the centre. The transformation into Cui (萃, Gathering Together) resolves the isolation: the scattered assemble, the crowd forms.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
Constraint experienced in the midst of apparent abundance, with recognition arriving late and action explicitly discouraged, resolving into assembly. The flavour is of being stuck while surrounded by the appearance of comfort, and of relief coming through gathering rather than through effort.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- A marsh with the water drained out from under it: the container intact, the contents gone.
- Oppressed among wine and food: difficulty that plenty does not solve.
- The vermilion sash on its way: honour approaching but not yet conferred.
- Words not believed: the specific frustration of this hexagram — the message does not land.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
征凶 is explicit: initiating action here is ominous, and the favourable act is ceremonial rather than practical. The surprise is the setting — Oppression usually reads as deprivation, and this line places it squarely inside abundance. Expect a difficulty that is not about lack of resources, and a recognition that arrives just behind the moment it was needed.
