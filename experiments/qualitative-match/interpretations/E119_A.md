1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Earth above wind: Sheng, Pushing Upward. Wood growing up through packed earth — ascent by persistence rather than by force. The judgment recommends seeing the great person and advancing south. The body trigram controls the use trigram: the subject has the advantage over the situation.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Kun above: earth, the resistant mass being pushed through.
- Xun below: wood, the patient growing thing.
- The fourth line is correct in place but has no answering line, and rests on a firm line — the unstable arrangement. Its text: "王用亨于岐山，吉无咎" — the king makes an offering on Mount Qi; auspicious, no blame. Mount Qi is the ancestral seat of the Zhou, the place from which the dynasty rose.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc is a steady climb, and at the fourth line it pauses for a rite. The offering is made not at the summit but at the ancestral mountain — the place of origin, not of arrival. This is an act of acknowledgment: the ascent is credited to where it came from rather than to the one making it. The mutual hexagram Gui Mei (歸妹, The Marrying Maiden) marks entry into an arrangement on terms not one's own. The transformation into Heng (恆, Duration) — thunder above wind, the enduring configuration — converts the ascent into something that simply persists: not rising further, not falling, continuing.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
An ascent formally acknowledged at a place of origin, favourable and blameless, resolving into steady continuation. The flavour is ceremonial and settled — a climb that stops climbing and becomes a durable state, marked by a rite rather than by a victory.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- Wood forcing its way up through earth: growth that succeeds by not stopping.
- The king's offering on the ancestral mountain: a rite performed at the origin, not the destination.
- The marrying maiden: entry on someone else's terms.
- Thunder and wind together: the pairing that lasts, in the hexagram of duration.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
The line has no correspondent, so the ceremony is performed without an answering party — it is self-directed acknowledgment. The surprise is the destination: Pushing Upward, a hexagram entirely about ascent, resolves into Duration, which is about staying the same. The climb converts into a plateau rather than a peak.
