MUTUAL: 雷澤歸妹

1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Earth above wind: Sheng, Pushing Upward. Wood growing under the earth and forcing its way up — the hexagram of steady, effortful ascent through resistance. The judgment: "用見大人，勿恤，南征吉", it is favourable to see the great person, do not worry, advancing south is auspicious. The body trigram controls the use trigram: the subject has the advantage.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Kun above: earth, the mass being pushed through.
- Xun below: wood, the growing thing that will not stop.
- The top line is correct in place with a proper correspondent. Its text: "冥升，利于不息之貞" — ascending in darkness; it is favourable to persist without ceasing. 冥 is blind, unlit, the ascent continued past the point where anything can be seen.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc is one of pushing up through resistance and continuing past the useful limit. At the top the climb has entered the dark — the subject is still ascending, but no longer able to see where. The text's counsel is curious and double-edged: the only thing that helps now is unceasing persistence, which is also precisely what got the position into darkness. The mutual hexagram Gui Mei (歸妹, The Marrying Maiden) marks entry into an arrangement on someone else's terms. The transformation into Gu (蠱, Work on the Decayed) — the bowl with worms in it — names the destination: something has rotted while attention was elsewhere, and now must be cleaned up.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
An ascent carried past its own visibility, sustained only by momentum, resolving into repair of accumulated neglect. The flavour is of climbing in the dark because stopping is not in the repertoire, and of discovering at the top that something below has spoiled.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- Wood forcing upward through packed earth: growth that works by persistence, not force.
- Ascending blind: the climb continued past the last point of orientation.
- The marrying maiden: entry into a structure on terms one did not set.
- A vessel gone rotten: decay found only after the climb.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
Sheng is a favourable hexagram, but the top line is where favour turns ambiguous — the same virtue that carried the ascent becomes the thing that will not let it stop. The surprise is the transformation into Gu: an upward story ending in remediation. Look for the achievement that reveals a neglected problem rather than crowning the effort.
