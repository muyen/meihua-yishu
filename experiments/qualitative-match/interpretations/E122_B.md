1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Mountain above heaven: Da Xu, the Great Accumulating. Heaven held inside the mountain — enormous creative force contained by stillness. The judgment favours firmness and says it is auspicious not to eat at home: the stored strength is meant to be spent outside. The use trigram feeds the body trigram: the situation nourishes the subject.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Gen above: the mountain, the container.
- Qian below: heaven, the force being stored.
- The fourth line is correct in place with a proper correspondent, but rests on a firm line — the unstable soft-over-hard arrangement. Its text: "童牛之牿，元吉" — the headboard on a young bull; supreme good fortune. The 牿 is the wooden bar fixed across a calf's horns before they have grown.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc is restraint applied to great force, and the fourth line is where the restraint is applied at exactly the right moment. The bull is young, the horns are not yet dangerous, and the guard goes on early — before there is any struggle, before the force is capable of resisting. The verdict is 元吉, the strongest in the vocabulary, and it belongs to the timing rather than to the restraint itself. Restraint applied later would be a fight; applied now it costs nothing. The mutual hexagram Gui Mei (歸妹, The Marrying Maiden) marks entry on terms not one's own. The transformation into Da You (大有, Possession in Great Measure) converts the containment into abundance: the sun at the zenith over great holdings.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
A constraint imposed early and painlessly on something that would later be difficult to constrain, earning the highest verdict, resolving into visible abundance. The flavour is of a small preventive act taken in time and of the eventual outcome being far larger than the act.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- The board fixed across a calf's horns before they grow: prevention applied before there is anything to prevent.
- Heaven contained inside the mountain: vast energy inside something immobile.
- The marrying maiden: entry into an arrangement on someone else's terms.
- The sun at noon over great holdings: everything visible and everything held.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
The structural instability of the position sits oddly against 元吉 — the seat is not secure and the outcome is nonetheless the best available. The surprise is where the good fortune comes from: not from strength or from resistance, but purely from acting early. Expect a modest, timely measure to produce a disproportionately favourable result.
