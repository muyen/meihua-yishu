HEXAGRAM LABEL: A
PRIMARY: 61 風澤中孚 -> CHANGED: 60 水澤節 (Line 6)
MUTUAL: 山雷頤

1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Wind above lake: Zhong Fu, Inner Truth. Two yielding lines at the centre with firm lines above and below — the shape of an empty heart, sincerity with nothing solid at the core. The judgment: "豚魚吉", even pigs and fish are moved; it is favourable to cross the great water. The body trigram controls the use trigram.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Xun above: wind, penetrating influence.
- Dui below: the lake, open and receptive.
- The top line is incorrect in place though it has a correspondent. Its text: "翰音登于天，貞凶" — the crowing of a cockerel rises to heaven; persistence is ominous. The sound climbs where the bird cannot follow. A rooster's call reaching the sky is the classic image of reputation exceeding substance.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc of Inner Truth runs from genuine sincerity toward its counterfeit. At the top the sound has separated from the thing making it: the crowing ascends, the cockerel stays on the ground. This is the failure mode specific to a hexagram built on an empty centre — when the emptiness stops being receptivity and becomes hollowness. The verdict 貞凶 says that persisting in this position is where the damage lies. The mutual hexagram Yi (頤, Nourishment) sits at the centre. The transformation into Jie (節, Limitation) supplies the correction: the bounded basin, the joint in the bamboo, a measure imposed on what had risen past its measure.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
Something whose noise has outrun its substance, warned against persisting, resolving into the imposition of limits. The flavour is of claim exceeding reality and of a ceiling arriving to enforce the difference. Not disaster — a correction downward toward the actual size of things.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- The rooster's crow climbing into the sky while the bird stays in the yard.
- An empty centre: hollowness where receptivity was.
- Pigs and fish moved by sincerity: the reach of genuine trust, in the judgment this line falls away from.
- The bamboo joint: the natural stop that determines how far anything grows.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
This is one of the book's sharpest warnings about reputation outpacing substance, and the 凶 attaches specifically to continuing rather than to the state itself. The surprise is the transformation into Limitation: the resolution is not collapse but measurement. Expect something to be cut back to its true size rather than to fail outright.
