HEXAGRAM LABEL: B
PRIMARY: 59 風水渙 -> CHANGED: 20 風地觀 (Line 2)
MUTUAL: 山雷頤

1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Wind above water: Huan, Dispersion. Wind breaking up the surface of water — dissolution, the loosening of what has hardened. The judgment favours crossing the great water. The use trigram feeds the body trigram: the situation nourishes the subject.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Xun as body: wind, the dispersing agent.
- Kan as use: water, the depth and the danger.
- The second line is central but incorrect in place and unanswered, and sits over a yielding line. Its text: "渙奔其机，悔亡" — in dispersion he hurries to his support; regret disappears. 机 is a low bench or armrest — the thing one leans on. Amid general scattering, this position moves fast toward something solid.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc is one of things coming apart, and the second line's response is speed toward support rather than resistance. The verb 奔 is running, urgent movement — this is not a considered repositioning but a dash. What is reached is modest: not a fortress, a bench. And that is enough for the regret to dissolve. The mutual hexagram Yi (頤, Nourishment) sits at the centre. The transformation into Guan (觀, Contemplation) — wind over earth, the tower from which one surveys — converts urgent movement into standing and looking.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
A rapid move toward modest support during a general breakup, which succeeds, resolving into observation. The flavour is of reacting fast to something dissolving, grabbing whatever is solid, and then a period of watching from a distance once the movement stops.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- Wind scattering the surface of water: cohesion breaking up from outside.
- Running to a bench: reaching for something to lean on rather than something to defend.
- Regret dissolving: a fear that turns out not to be realised.
- The tower and the surveying gaze: distance replacing urgency.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
The position is unsupported by any correspondent, so the support it reaches is found rather than provided. The surprise is the modesty of what suffices — the line's remedy is a piece of furniture, not an ally or a stronghold. Expect the thing that stabilises the situation to be smaller and more ordinary than the disturbance would suggest.
