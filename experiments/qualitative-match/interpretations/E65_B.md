EVENT: Commonwealth Games athletics — closing night relays
HEXAGRAM LABEL: B
PRIMARY: 54 雷澤歸妹 -> CHANGED: 19 地澤臨 (Line 4)
MUTUAL: 水火既濟

1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Thunder above lake: Gui Mei, The Marrying Maiden. The younger sister entering a household in a subordinate role — the hexagram of joining an arrangement on terms one did not set. The judgment is one of the bluntest in the book: "征凶，无攸利", to advance is ominous, nothing is favourable. The body trigram controls the use trigram.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Zhen above: thunder, the movement that initiates.
- Dui below: the lake, the joy that attracts and the position that yields.
- The fourth line is incorrect in place and unanswered, and rests over a yielding line. Its text: "歸妹愆期，遲歸有時" — the marrying maiden misses the appointed date; a late marriage comes in its own time. A schedule is missed, and the line declines to call it a failure.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc concerns an entry made from a position of limited leverage, and at the fourth line the timing slips. 愆期 is specifically the missing of a set date — the arrangement was scheduled and it did not happen when it was supposed to. The second clause supplies the reading: it will happen, later, when its own time arrives. There is no verdict character attached, which in a hexagram this discouraging is close to reassurance. The mutual hexagram Ji Ji (既濟, After Completion) holds full order at the centre. The transformation into Lin (臨, Approach) — something large drawing near, with a favourable judgment and a stated expiry — turns the delay into an advance.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
A missed schedule that is not a cancellation, resolving into a confident approach. The flavour is of something not happening on the announced date and of that slippage turning out to be the mechanism by which it happens properly. Delay as timing, not as failure.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- The bride whose wedding date passes without the wedding.
- Waiting for one's own time rather than the appointed one.
- Thunder over a lake: movement above, yielding below.
- Something large drawing near: the approach that arrives under its own momentum.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
Gui Mei's overall judgment is as negative as the book gets, so the neutrality of this line stands out sharply against it. The surprise is that the delay is the favourable element: in a hexagram where advancing is explicitly ominous, the line that fails to proceed on schedule is the one that comes out ahead. Expect a postponement that improves rather than damages the outcome.
