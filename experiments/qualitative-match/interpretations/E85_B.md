EVENT: Meta quarterly earnings and next-day stock reaction
HEXAGRAM LABEL: B
PRIMARY: 60 水澤節 -> CHANGED: 5 水天需 (Line 3)
MUTUAL: 山雷頤

1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Water above lake: Jie, Limitation. A vessel with a fixed capacity and something flowing into it — the hexagram of the joint in a bamboo stalk, the notch that measures. The judgment warns that limitation itself can be overdone: "苦節不可貞", bitter limitation cannot be persisted in. The use trigram feeds the body: the situation nourishes the subject.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Kan as body: water, danger, depth, what flows and finds its level.
- Dui as use: the lake, the bounded basin, the container.
- The third line is incorrect, unanswered, and sits above a firm line — the least stable configuration. Its text: "不節若，則嗟若，无咎" — if one does not limit oneself, one will lament; no blame. The failure of restraint is named outright, and the sighing that follows, and then — unexpectedly — the blame is withdrawn.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc concerns something exceeding its measure. At the third line restraint is not exercised, and the immediate consequence is regret. The remarkable turn is 无咎: the lament is real, but no lasting fault attaches. The mutual hexagram Yi (頤, Nourishment) — the open jaws — sits at the centre: the question of what is taken in and how much. The transformation into Xu (需, Waiting) resolves the overflow into patience: clouds rise to heaven and one waits for rain, eating and drinking in the meantime. Excess converts into a holding pattern.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
A measure exceeded, regretted, and then absorbed without lasting damage, ending in an extended wait. The flavour is of overshooting, feeling it, and then discovering there is nothing to do but sit with it for a while. Not punished; not resolved either.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- The joint in the bamboo: the natural stop that gives a stalk its strength.
- A lake taking on more water than it holds: overflow at the rim.
- Sighing after the fact: regret that arrives too late to prevent anything.
- Clouds gathered and waiting for rain: everything in place, nothing yet falling.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
Jie warns in both directions — too little limitation and too much are both faulted, and this hexagram is unusual in saying so explicitly. The surprise is the 无咎 attached to a clear failure of restraint: the text forgives the overshoot. The third line's instability suggests the excess is structural rather than a lapse of will.
