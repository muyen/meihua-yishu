EVENT: Missouri primary elections
HEXAGRAM LABEL: A
PRIMARY: 15 地山謙 -> CHANGED: 62 雷山小過 (Line 4)
MUTUAL: 雷水解

1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Earth above mountain: Qian, Modesty — the mountain beneath the ground, the only hexagram in which every line is favourable. The judgment: the noble person carries things to completion. Body and use are in 比和, like natures in harmony: no conflict, and no generative push either.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Kun above: earth, level, undemonstrative.
- Gen below: the mountain, vast and out of sight.
- The fourth line is correct in place but unanswered, and rests on a firm line — the unstable soft-over-hard arrangement. Its text: "无不利，撝謙" — nothing that is not favourable; modesty displayed outward. 撝 is a gesture of the hand, a directing or waving motion: modesty that is actively signalled rather than merely held.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc concerns lowness maintained in an elevated position. The fourth place sits directly beneath the ruler and is conventionally the most exposed seat in the figure — proximity to power without the authority. The response here is to make the modesty visible, to wave it outward as a deliberate act. The blanket verdict 无不利 covers everything. The mutual hexagram Jie (解, Deliverance) indicates a knot being loosened. The transformation into Xiao Guo (小過, Small Exceeding) — thunder over mountain, the bird that should stay low — reduces the scale of the whole configuration.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
A deliberately deferential posture held in a exposed position, with nothing going wrong, resolving into something smaller than its frame. The flavour is of correct conduct producing an absence of trouble rather than a positive gain, and of the closing register being modest in scope.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- A mountain hidden beneath flat ground.
- Modesty waved outward with the hand: deference performed so it can be seen.
- The seat next to the throne: maximum exposure, minimum authority.
- The bird that should not fly high: the counsel to keep things small.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
All six lines of Qian are favourable, so the absence of warning is structural. The distinctive note is that this position's safety is achieved by demonstration — the modesty has to be seen to work. The surprise is the transformation into Small Exceeding: the outcome contracts. Expect nothing to go wrong and nothing to be large.
