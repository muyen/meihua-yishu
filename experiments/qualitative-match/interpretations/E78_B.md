EVENT: National Bank Open, Toronto
HEXAGRAM LABEL: B
PRIMARY: 33 天山遯 -> CHANGED: 53 風山漸 (Line 4)
MUTUAL: 天風姤

1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Heaven above mountain: Dun, Retreat. The mountain reaches up and heaven withdraws further — the hexagram of strategic withdrawal, of yielding ground while the yielding is still voluntary. The judgment: "小利貞", in small matters it is favourable to persist. The body trigram drains into the use trigram: the subject expends itself.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Qian above: heaven, receding upward and out of reach.
- Gen below: the mountain, the fixed thing that stays.
- The fourth line is incorrect in place but has a correspondent. Its text: "好遯，君子吉，小人否" — a willing retreat; for the noble person, auspicious; for the small person, not. The same act splits into two opposite outcomes depending entirely on who performs it.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc is a withdrawal executed in good order. 好遯 is retreat undertaken willingly, without resentment and without being forced — the character 好 carries the sense of doing it well and gladly. The line then makes an unusually explicit distinction: the identical move is favourable for one kind of actor and unfavourable for another. What separates them is the ability to let go of what is being left. The mutual hexagram Gou (姤, Coming to Meet) marks an unplanned entry inside the process. The transformation into Jian (漸, Gradual Progress) — the wild goose advancing stage by stage — converts retreat into measured forward movement.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
A voluntary withdrawal that reads as success for whoever can accept it and as failure for whoever cannot, resolving into slow patient advance. The flavour is of stepping back on one's own terms and of the outcome being determined by disposition rather than by circumstance.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- Heaven withdrawing above an unmoving mountain: distance opening because one party rises away.
- Retreat done gladly: the withdrawal that costs nothing because nothing is clung to.
- The same door read as exit by one and as defeat by another.
- The wild goose advancing in stages: progress that cannot be hurried.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
Dun's counsel is that timing is everything — retreat while it is still a choice. The fourth line's split verdict is its distinctive feature: this is one of the few lines in the book whose outcome depends explicitly on the character of the actor rather than the shape of the situation. Expect the same event to be read as a good outcome by one party and a bad one by another.
