EVENT: Commonwealth Games artistic gymnastics
HEXAGRAM LABEL: B
PRIMARY: 9 風天小畜 -> CHANGED: 5 水天需 (Line 6)
MUTUAL: 火澤睽

1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Wind above heaven: Xiao Xu, the Small Accumulating, read at the top line where the restraint has finally done its work. The judgment's image is dense clouds without rain; by the sixth line the rain has come. The body trigram controls the use trigram: the subject holds the check.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Xun above: wind, the gentle restraining influence.
- Qian below: heaven, the great force being held.
- The top line is incorrect in place and unanswered. Its text is the longest in the hexagram: "既雨既處，尚德載，婦貞厲。月幾望，君子征凶" — it has rained and settled; virtue is carried to the full; for the wife, persistence brings danger. The moon is nearly full; for the noble person, to march out is ominous.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc completes: the accumulated pressure discharges as rain and things come to rest. But the line immediately converts arrival into warning. 月幾望 — the moon almost full — is the classic image of a maximum that must not be pushed further, because the next phase after fullness is waning. Two explicit prohibitions follow: persistence is dangerous for the one holding the position, and setting out is ominous for the one who would advance. The mutual hexagram Kui (睽, Opposition) marks divergence inside the situation. The transformation into Xu (需, Waiting) — clouds above heaven — returns everything to a holding pattern.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
An accumulation that finally discharges, immediately followed by a double warning against pressing on, resolving into waiting. The flavour is of something arriving at exactly its high-water mark, with the text insisting that this is the moment to stop rather than continue.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- Rain after long dense cloud: the release that was withheld through the whole hexagram.
- The moon just short of full: the maximum, one step before the turn.
- Virtue carried to the full load: a cart at its capacity.
- Clouds gathered again over heaven: back to waiting, with nothing falling.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
This line carries more explicit warnings than any other in the hexagram, and they arrive immediately after the successful outcome. The structure is: it worked — now stop. The surprise is that the transformation is into Waiting rather than into any form of consolidation. The discharge does not settle anything permanently; the whole configuration re-forms and the waiting begins again.
