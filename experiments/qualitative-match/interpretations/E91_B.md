EVENT: Guam primary election
HEXAGRAM LABEL: B
PRIMARY: 55 雷火豐 -> CHANGED: 36 地火明夷 (Line 4)
MUTUAL: 澤風大過

1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Thunder above fire: Feng, Abundance. The hexagram of maximum brightness and maximum noise — and, uniquely, of the moment just before decline. The judgment carries an unusual note of caution at the peak: "日中則昃", the sun at noon begins to sink. The use trigram Li (fire) feeds the body trigram Zhen (wood): the situation actively fuels the subject's movement.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Zhen as body: sudden motion, the foot, the thing that springs.
- Li as use: light, spectacle, everything on display.
- The fourth line is incorrect in its place and has no answering line. Its text: "豐其蔀，日中見斗，遇其夷主，吉" — the screen is so thick that at midday one sees the Dipper; but one meets an equal, and it is auspicious. Broad daylight has been curtained into darkness, and the resolution comes from an encounter with a peer rather than a superior.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc is at full blaze and something occludes it. Seeing the Dipper at noon is a total inversion: the stars come out in the middle of the day. Into that obscurity walks a 夷主 — a host of equal rank, not a patron. The mutual hexagram Da Guo (大過, Great Exceeding) is the ridgepole bending under load, the structural warning at the centre. The transformation into Ming Yi (明夷) confirms the direction: brightness wounded, the light going under. Abundance does not consolidate here; it dims.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
A peak that is reached and then curtained. The character is emphatically not failure — the line says 吉, and the meeting between equals is genuine — but the trajectory is downward from a high point. The taste is of a great occasion that ends earlier or darker than its opening promised.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- The Dipper visible at noon: stars in a daylight sky, the world turned around.
- A thick screen over a bright hall: obstruction placed in front of what was meant to shine.
- Meeting one's equal: recognition between peers, no hierarchy involved.
- The ridgepole flexing: the load carried is near the structure's limit.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
Feng is the hexagram that warns at its own summit; the danger is not scarcity but excess. The fourth line's lack of a correspondent means no support arrives from above — whatever is settled is settled between equals. The surprise is how fast the light goes: a single moving line converts full Abundance into Darkening of the Light.
