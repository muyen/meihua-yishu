EVENT: Frontier model release window
HEXAGRAM LABEL: A
PRIMARY: 13 天火同人 -> CHANGED: 37 風火家人 (Line 4)
MUTUAL: 天風姤

1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Heaven above fire: Tong Ren, Fellowship with Others. Fire rising toward heaven, moving in the same direction as what is above it — the hexagram of common cause. Its judgment places the fellowship in the open: "同人于野", fellowship in the open country, where it is favourable to cross the great water. The body trigram controls the use trigram.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Qian above: heaven, the shared aim everyone is oriented toward.
- Li below: fire, brightness, clarity about who is with whom.
- The fourth line is incorrect in place and unanswered. Its text: "乘其墉，弗克攻，吉" — he mounts the wall but cannot attack; auspicious. The position is taken, the assault is not carried out, and the verdict is favourable.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc concerns parties finding common ground, and the fourth line is where a conflict does not happen. Someone climbs onto the rampart — the aggressive move is fully prepared, the height is gained — and then the attack does not go in. 弗克 can be read as unable or as failing to; either way the assault is not delivered, and the book calls this auspicious. The favourable outcome belongs to the attack not happening. The mutual hexagram Gou (姤, Coming to Meet) marks an unplanned entry. The transformation into Jia Ren (家人, The Family) resolves the fellowship inward: the open-country alliance becomes an ordered household.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
An aggressive position taken and then not used, with the restraint producing the good outcome, resolving into ordered internal structure. The flavour is of a confrontation that is set up and called off, and of the parties ending up more closely bound than before it.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- Standing on top of the wall without going over it: the attack prepared and withheld.
- Fire rising toward heaven: two things moving the same way.
- Fellowship in open country rather than behind gates.
- The household: an alliance that becomes a structure with roles.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
The fourth line has no correspondent, so whatever restraint occurs is not the result of negotiation. The surprise is the verdict: a failed attack is called 吉, which inverts the normal reading of an unsuccessful assault. Expect a confrontation that does not occur, and expect that to be the favourable development rather than the anticlimax.
