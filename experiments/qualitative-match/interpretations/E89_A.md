1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Lake above water: Kun, Oppression. The lake drained dry, its water sunk below where it can be reached — exhaustion, and the specific frustration that words carry no weight. The body trigram drains into the use trigram: the subject expends itself.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Dui above: the empty basin.
- Kan below: water gone to the depths, danger.
- The third line is incorrect in place, unanswered, and rests on a firm line — the least stable arrangement available. Its text is among the bleakest in the book: "困于石，據于蒺藜，入于其宮，不見其妻，凶" — oppressed by a stone, resting on thorns; entering his house, he does not see his wife; ominous.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc is a sequence of positions none of which offer relief. A stone blocks the way; the place to sit is thorns; and the refuge — the house, the last recourse — is empty. The structure of the line is three attempts at rest, each worse than the last, terminating in absence rather than in attack. The mutual hexagram Jia Ren (家人, The Family) holds the ordered household at the centre, which sharpens the irony: the interior order exists in the middle of the figure and is precisely what is missing at the end of the line. The transformation into Da Guo (大過, Great Exceeding) — the ridgepole bending under load — leaves the structure straining.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
Repeated attempts to find footing, each failing, ending in an absence where support was expected, and resolving into structural overload. The flavour is of exhaustion compounded by the discovery that the fallback is not there — the harm is what is missing rather than what arrives.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- A stone in the way and thorns to sit on: no position that is not painful.
- Coming home to an empty house: the refuge that has been vacated.
- Words not believed: the hexagram's signature frustration.
- The ridgepole flexing: weight beyond what the beam was built for.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
This is one of the most unambiguously 凶 lines in the book, and its structural position compounds it — incorrect, unanswered, unstable. The surprise is the nature of the misfortune: nothing attacks. The damage is entirely negative — what should be present is not. Expect a loss that shows up as an absence rather than as an event.
