HEXAGRAM LABEL: A
PRIMARY: 35 火地晉 -> CHANGED: 23 山地剝 (Line 4)
MUTUAL: 水山蹇

1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Fire above earth: Jin, Progress. The sun rising over open ground — advancement, increasing light, rank conferred. The judgment describes a prince receiving horses in quantity and being granted audience three times in one day. The use trigram feeds the body trigram: the situation nourishes the subject.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Li above: fire, the sun climbing.
- Kun below: earth, the open ground the light crosses.
- The fourth line is incorrect in place, has a correspondent, and rests over a yielding line. Its text: "晉如鼫鼠，貞厲" — advancing like a large rodent; persistence is dangerous. The 鼫鼠 is the creature of many small abilities and no complete one — it climbs, swims, digs and burrows, all badly.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc is one of rising, and at the fourth line the manner of rising is indicted. The rodent advances by nibbling and hoarding rather than by merit, occupying a position it cannot properly fill. The fourth seat is directly beneath the ruler — maximum exposure, minimum authority — and the warning 貞厲 says that holding this position is where the danger lies. The mutual hexagram Jian (蹇, Obstruction) sits at the centre: hard travelling concealed inside a story of advancement. The transformation into Bo (剝, Splitting Apart) confirms the trajectory: support stripped away rung by rung from below.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
An advance achieved by accumulation rather than merit, occupying an exposed seat, warned against persisting, resolving into progressive erosion. The flavour is of something rising further than its foundation justifies, and of the ground being taken out from under it afterwards.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- The rodent with five skills and no mastery: capability spread too thin to be capable.
- The sun climbing over flat ground while the going underneath is hard.
- The seat beneath the throne: exposure without authority.
- The bed stripped from the legs upward: support removed methodically.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
The warning is 貞厲, danger in persistence — the position is not condemned outright, but staying in it is. The surprise is the contrast between the hexagram and the line: Jin is a straightforwardly favourable figure about advancement, and this line is its sharpest internal criticism. Expect a rise that is real and a foundation that is not.
