EVENT: MLB trade deadline
HEXAGRAM LABEL: A
PRIMARY: 19 地澤臨 -> CHANGED: 24 地雷復 (Line 2)
MUTUAL: 地雷復

1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Earth above lake: Lin, Approach. Something large is drawing near — two firm lines advancing from below under four yielding ones. The judgment is strongly favourable but time-stamped: "至于八月有凶", by the eighth month there will be misfortune. Approach carries an expiry date. The body trigram drains into the use trigram: the subject expends itself on the situation.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Kun as body: earth, the receptive ground being approached.
- Dui as use: the lake, joy, the open surface the advance comes across.
- The second line is central, incorrect in place, but has a firm correspondent. Its text: "咸臨，吉无不利" — approach through mutual influence; auspicious, nothing that is not favourable. This is one of the least qualified positive statements in the entire book: no warning clause attached.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc is one of confident advance. The second line's 咸 is the character for reciprocal feeling — the approach works because both sides are moved, not because one imposes on the other. The mutual hexagram is Fu (復, Return), and the transformation is also into Fu: thunder within the earth, the single yang returning at the bottom, the turning point of the year. The situation resolves not into expansion but into a fresh start from the ground up. Everything folds back to a beginning.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
An advance that succeeds without reservation and then returns to its starting point. The dominant flavour is genuinely favourable — the line is unusually unhedged — but the destination is a beginning rather than a culmination. Something is completed and immediately restarts.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- The lake beneath the earth's rim: something rising to meet what stands above it.
- Mutual influence rather than pressure: both parties moved, neither forced.
- The single unbroken line returning at the bottom: light coming back at the darkest point of the year.
- The eighth month written into the judgment: a favourable state with a date on it.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
The warning is not in the line but in the hexagram's own judgment — Lin's favour is explicitly temporary, and the text names the deadline. The surprise is the doubled Fu: both the mutual and the changed hexagram are Return, so every reading of the middle and end of this situation points to the same thing — a cycle closing and reopening rather than a gain being banked.
