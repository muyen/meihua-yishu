EVENT: Microsoft quarterly earnings
HEXAGRAM LABEL: A
PRIMARY: 44 天風姤 -> CHANGED: 50 火風鼎 (Line 5)
MUTUAL: 乾為天

1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Heaven above wind: Gou, Coming to Meet — a single yielding line entering beneath five firm ones. The hexagram of the unplanned encounter, of something small arriving underneath a structure that looked settled. The use trigram attacks the body trigram: the situation cuts against the subject.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Qian above: five unbroken lines, imposing and rigid.
- Xun below: the soft line that has come in from underneath.
- The fifth line holds the sovereign position and is correct in place, but has no answering line. Its text: "以杞包瓜，含章，有隕自天" — a melon wrapped in willow leaves; the pattern is held within; something falls from heaven. Concealment of what is valuable, and then an arrival nobody arranged.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc concerns something entering unbidden and how the ruling position responds. The response is to cover: a melon — sweet, ripening, vulnerable — is wrapped in leaves rather than displayed. 含章 is the holding-in of pattern, excellence kept out of sight. Then 有隕自天: a thing drops from the sky. The character 隕 is a fall, not a gift; it is unsolicited and unannounced. The mutual hexagram is Qian doubled, unrelieved hardness through the middle. The transformation into Ding (鼎, the Cauldron) turns the whole configuration into a vessel of transformation — raw material becoming something else under contained heat.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
Value deliberately concealed rather than shown, followed by an unearned arrival from above, resolving into transformation. The flavour is of strength held back from display, and of the decisive factor coming from outside the frame entirely rather than from any party's effort.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- A melon wrapped in willow leaves: something ripe and worth having, kept covered.
- Pattern held within: excellence that does not announce itself.
- Something falling from heaven: an arrival with no visible cause and no author.
- The cauldron over the fire: ingredients turning into a different substance.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
The fifth line has no correspondent, so the ruling position acts without support — the concealment is a solitary decision. The surprise is 有隕自天, one of the very few line texts in the book that introduces a purely exogenous event: the decisive thing is not done by anyone in the picture. Expect an outside factor to matter more than either party's manoeuvring.
