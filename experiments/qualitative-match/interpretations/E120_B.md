EVENT: Geomagnetic storm window
HEXAGRAM LABEL: B
PRIMARY: 21 火雷噬嗑 -> CHANGED: 30 離為火 (Line 3)
MUTUAL: 水山蹇

1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Fire above thunder: Shi He, Biting Through. Lightning and thunder together — the hexagram of jaws closing on an obstruction lodged between them. The judgment: "亨，利用獄", success; favourable for legal proceedings. Something is in the way and must be removed by force rather than negotiated around. The use trigram feeds the body trigram.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Li above: fire, lightning, the clarity that reveals what is stuck.
- Zhen below: thunder, the force that closes.
- The third line is incorrect in place but has a correspondent. Its text: "噬臘肉，遇毒，小吝，无咎" — biting dried meat and meeting poison; slight regret; no blame. The obstruction is bitten through, and something unpleasant is encountered in the process.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc is the removal of what blocks. At the third line the bite goes through cured meat — tough, preserved, long-kept — and encounters something spoiled inside it. The two verdicts that follow are carefully graded: 小吝, a small regret, and then 无咎, no blame. The unpleasantness is real and minor, and no fault attaches to the one who bit. The mutual hexagram Jian (蹇, Obstruction) — water above mountain, the hardest travelling in the book — sits at the centre. The transformation into Li (離) doubled, fire above fire, resolves into pure brightness: everything illuminated, nothing concealed.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
An obstruction bitten through with something unpleasant discovered inside it, minor regret, no lasting fault, resolving into full exposure. The flavour is of dealing with something long-preserved and finding it has gone off — and of the aftermath being total visibility rather than concealment.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- Dried, cured meat: something kept a long time before anyone examined it.
- Meeting poison mid-bite: the unpleasant thing found inside, not outside.
- Lightning and thunder together: illumination and force arriving in the same instant.
- Fire doubled: light with nothing left to hide behind.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
The graded verdicts are the distinctive feature — the book takes care to say the regret is small and the blame is nil. The surprise is where the trouble is located: not in resistance from the obstruction but in its contents. Expect something that has been sitting untouched to turn out to be spoiled, and expect the discovery to cost little.
