1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Mountain above earth: Bo, Splitting Apart, read at the top line — the single firm line left standing above five yielding ones, and the end of the stripping process. Body and use are in 比和, like natures. The judgment for the hexagram as a whole is not to go anywhere.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Gen above: the last remaining firm line, the fruit at the top of the tree.
- Kun below: earth, entirely yielding, having risen through the whole figure.
- The top line is incorrect in place but has a correspondent, and rests over a yielding line — the orderly firm-over-soft relation. Its text: "碩果不食，君子得輿，小人剝廬" — the great fruit is not eaten; the noble person obtains a carriage; the small person's hut is stripped away.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc of stripping completes and something survives it: one large fruit left uneaten on the branch, which is also the seed for the next cycle. The line then splits sharply along the same axis as the rest of the book: the same conditions deliver a carriage to one kind of actor and take the roof off another. The mutual hexagram is Kun doubled — pure yielding through the middle. The transformation into Kun (坤) proper, six broken lines, completes the removal: the last firm line goes and nothing but receptive earth remains.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
The end of a stripping process in which one thing is preserved as seed while the same events deliver mobility to one party and shelter loss to another, resolving into complete yieldingness. The flavour is of a hard ending that is not total — something is kept back — with sharply divergent consequences for different positions.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- The great fruit left uneaten: the seed that survives the harvest, the last thing not consumed.
- A carriage obtained: mobility and standing arriving for one party.
- A hut stripped of its roof: the same event removing another party's shelter.
- Six broken lines: pure receptivity, nothing firm left anywhere.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
Bo's counsel throughout is not to initiate, and the top line is the only one that carries a positive image. The surprise is its double edge: this is one of the sharpest 君子/小人 splits in the book, where a single set of facts produces opposite outcomes for different actors. Expect the same event to be a gain and a loss simultaneously depending on where one stands.
