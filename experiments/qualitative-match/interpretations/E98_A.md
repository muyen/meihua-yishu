EVENT: Hawaii primary election
HEXAGRAM LABEL: A
PRIMARY: 64 火水未濟 -> CHANGED: 35 火地晉 (Line 2)
MUTUAL: 水火既濟

1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Fire above water: Wei Ji, Before Completion. The last hexagram in the sequence, and the one in which every single line sits in the wrong place — fire rising, water sinking, the two never meeting. The judgment's image is the little fox that has almost crossed the river and wets its tail. The use trigram attacks the body trigram: the situation cuts against the subject.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Li above: fire, rising away from the water it should heat.
- Kan below: water, sinking away from the fire it should quench.
- The second line is central, incorrect in place, and has a correspondent. Its text: "曳其輪，貞吉" — he drags his wheels; persistence is auspicious. Dragging the wheels is braking: deliberately slowing a vehicle that would otherwise run away downhill.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc is an incomplete crossing, and the second line's contribution is restraint applied to one's own momentum. The image is mechanical and specific — a cart on a slope with its wheels locked, held back by friction rather than steered. The verdict attaches to holding, not to advancing. The mutual hexagram Ji Ji (既濟, After Completion) sits at the centre, which is the exact inverse of the outer figure: order in the middle of disorder. The transformation into Jin (晉, Progress) — the sun rising over the earth — releases the brake: advance, promotion, light increasing over open ground.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
Deliberate self-restraint in an unfinished situation, rewarded, and then converting into clear forward progress. The flavour is of holding back on purpose while everything is still out of position, and of the advance coming only after the restraint has been maintained.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- The little fox that wets its tail at the last step: almost across, and not across.
- Wheels dragged to brake a cart: momentum checked by friction, not by steering.
- Order hidden inside disorder: the middle of the figure fully arranged while the outside is not.
- The sun coming up over the ground: light spreading on a flat horizon.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
Wei Ji is the hexagram of not-yet, and its counsel is that the last step is where crossings fail. The elemental relation is the cutting one, so the pressure on the subject is real. The surprise is the direction of the transformation: the most disordered figure in the book resolves into unambiguous Progress. Expect a held-back phase followed by clear advance.
