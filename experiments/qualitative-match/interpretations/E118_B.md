EVENT: Atlantic hurricane season
HEXAGRAM LABEL: B
PRIMARY: 17 澤雷隨 -> CHANGED: 58 兌為澤 (Line 2)
MUTUAL: 風山漸

1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Lake above thunder: Sui, Following. Movement below, pleasure above — the hexagram of going along with what moves. Its judgment is fully favourable, but Following always poses the question of whom one follows. The body trigram controls the use trigram: the subject holds the advantage.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Dui above: the mouth, exchange, the pleasant surface.
- Zhen below: sudden movement, the initiating impulse.
- The second line is correct in place with a correspondent, but rests on a firm line — soft over hard, the unstable arrangement. Its text: "係小子，失丈夫" — one holds to the boy and loses the grown man. This is the exact inverse of the third line's trade: here the lesser attachment is chosen and the greater one forfeited.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc is one of allegiance, and at the second line the allegiance goes to the junior party. No verdict character is attached — the book neither approves nor condemns, it simply records that binding to the smaller thing costs the larger one. The two attachments are mutually exclusive; that is the whole content of the line. The mutual hexagram Jian (漸, Gradual Progress) says the shift happens by stages. The transformation into Dui (兌) doubled — lake above lake — resolves into pure open exchange, two bodies of water communicating with nothing between them.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
A choice that takes the nearer, smaller option at the cost of the larger one, recorded without judgment, resolving into open and easy exchange. The flavour is of a trade-off made in favour of what is immediately available, and of an aftermath that is sociable and unguarded rather than consequential.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- Holding the boy and letting go of the man: the smaller tie chosen, the greater one released.
- Thunder moving beneath a calm surface.
- Two attachments that cannot both be kept.
- Lake doubled: communication without any barrier remaining.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
The absence of a verdict character is itself the notable feature: the book usually says 吉 or 凶 and here it says nothing, leaving the trade as a plain fact. The structural instability of the position suggests the choice was not entirely free. The surprise is the pairing with the third line, which makes the opposite trade — the same hexagram contains both directions, and neither is condemned.
