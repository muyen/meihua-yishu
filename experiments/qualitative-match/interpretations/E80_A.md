EVENT: Commonwealth Games artistic gymnastics
HEXAGRAM LABEL: A
PRIMARY: 14 火天大有 -> CHANGED: 30 離為火 (Line 2)
MUTUAL: 澤天夬

1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Fire above heaven: Da You, Possession in Great Measure. The sun at the zenith over the creative — the hexagram of abundant holdings, where a single yielding line in the fifth place governs five firm ones. The judgment is 元亨, supremely successful. The body trigram controls the use trigram: the subject has the advantage.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Li above: fire, the sun in the middle of the sky, illuminating everything held.
- Qian below: heaven, the creative force underneath the wealth.
- The second line is incorrect in place but has a proper correspondent in the ruling fifth. Its text: "大車以載，有攸往，无咎" — a great wagon for carrying; there is somewhere to go; no blame. Capacity and destination stated together.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc is one of ample means properly employed. The second line's image is purely practical: a large cart, well built, loaded, and a place to take it. There is no drama in this line — it is the sober middle of an abundant hexagram, and its virtue is that the capacity is matched to a purpose. The mutual hexagram Guai (夬, Breakthrough) introduces a decisive rupture at the centre — five firm lines displacing a single soft one at the top. The transformation into Li (離) doubled — fire above fire — intensifies rather than resolves: pure brightness, clinging, radiance without any solid element remaining.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
Substantial capacity put to work toward a clear destination, escalating into pure display. The flavour is of a heavy load moved competently, and of the situation ending in something more luminous and less substantial than it began — brilliance where there had been cargo.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- A great wagon loaded and rolling: capacity matched to purpose.
- The sun directly overhead: everything visible, nothing in shadow.
- Breakthrough at the centre: a decisive clearing of the last obstruction.
- Fire doubled: light that has nothing left to burn but itself.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
Da You carries no serious warning at the second line — this is one of the more straightforwardly favourable positions in the book. The caution is structural rather than textual: the second line depends on its correspondent above, so the good outcome is relational, not self-generated. The surprise is the transformation into doubled fire: abundance resolving into radiance rather than into consolidation.
