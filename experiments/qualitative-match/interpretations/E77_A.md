1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Heaven above wind: Gou, Coming to Meet. A single yielding line has entered beneath five firm ones — the judgment warns "勿用取女", do not marry this maiden, because what has arrived looks small and is not. The use trigram attacks the body trigram (用克體): the situation cuts against the subject.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Qian above: five unbroken lines, the imposing structure.
- Xun below: the single soft line that has come in underneath everything.
- The fourth line is incorrect in place though it has a correspondent. Its text: "包无魚，起凶" — the wrapper contains no fish; setting out brings misfortune. The container that should have held something is empty, and acting on the assumption that it is full is the error the line names.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc begins with an unexpected small entry at the base of a strong structure. By the fourth line the position discovers its supply is gone: the fish that should be in the wrapping is not there. Crucially the fourth line's proper correspondent is the first line — the very soft line that entered — and that connection has been captured elsewhere. What was counted on has already attached to someone else. The mutual hexagram is Qian (乾) doubled: unrelieved hardness through the middle, no give anywhere. The transformation into Xun (巽) doubled removes the hardness entirely and leaves pure wind — repeated, penetrating, insubstantial.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
An empty container discovered at the moment of reaching for it, with the resource already committed elsewhere, resolving into something without substance. The flavour is of arriving to collect and finding the shelf bare — and of the loss being invisible from outside, because the wrapper looks the same either way.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- A wrapper with no fish in it: the package that looks full and is not.
- One soft line slipping in beneath five hard ones: the small thing that changes a structure from below.
- Doubled heaven at the centre: rigidity all the way through, nothing that bends.
- Wind above wind: influence with nothing solid behind it.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
The elemental relation is the cutting one and the line is explicitly 凶 on action — this is a position that should not initiate. The surprise is where the loss comes from: not from an opponent taking something, but from a supply that quietly attached itself elsewhere before anyone checked. Look for the resource that was assumed available and was already spoken for.
