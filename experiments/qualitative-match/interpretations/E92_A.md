EVENT: Michigan US Senate Democratic primary
HEXAGRAM LABEL: A
PRIMARY: 44 天風姤 -> CHANGED: 33 天山遯 (Line 2)
MUTUAL: 乾為天

1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Heaven above wind: Gou, Coming to Meet. One yielding line has entered beneath five firm ones — the arrival of something small into a structure that appeared complete. The judgment warns against taking it lightly. The body trigram controls the use trigram: the subject holds the advantage.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Qian above: five unbroken lines, the imposing frame.
- Xun below: the single soft line that has come in.
- The second line is central but incorrect in place and unanswered, and sits over a yielding line — firm above soft, the orderly relation. Its text: "包有魚，无咎，不利賓" — the wrapper contains a fish; no blame; not favourable for guests. The supply is in hand, and explicitly not to be shared with outsiders.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc opens with an unplanned entry, and the second line is the position that has secured it. The fish — the thing that entered — is wrapped and held here. The verdict is 无咎, correctness without reward, and then the pointed rider: 不利賓, this does not extend to guests. What has been secured is exclusive; attempting to distribute it is where the difficulty would begin. The mutual hexagram is Qian doubled, unrelieved rigidity through the middle. The transformation into Dun (遯, Retreat) — heaven withdrawing above the mountain — converts holding into stepping back.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
A resource secured and deliberately not shared, blameless but not generous, resolving into withdrawal. The flavour is of possession quietly held at the point of entry, with an explicit boundary drawn around who benefits, and of the position afterwards receding rather than pressing its advantage.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- A fish in the wrapping: the supply present and accounted for.
- The door closed to guests: a benefit that is not extended outward.
- One soft line beneath five hard ones: the small arrival that changes a structure.
- Heaven withdrawing above the mountain: distance opening deliberately.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
The pairing with the fourth line of the same hexagram is the point of interest: there the wrapper is empty and setting out is ominous; here it is full and the only caution concerns sharing. The surprise is the exclusivity clause — the book rarely says outright that a benefit should not be extended, and here it does.
