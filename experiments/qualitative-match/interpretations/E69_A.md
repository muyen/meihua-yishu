HEXAGRAM LABEL: A
PRIMARY: 17 澤雷隨 -> CHANGED: 49 澤火革 (Line 3)
MUTUAL: 風山漸

1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Lake above thunder: Sui, Following. Movement below, joy above — the hexagram of adaptation, of going along with what is moving. The judgment is fully favourable, 元亨利貞无咎, but Following carries an implicit question: following whom? The body trigram controls the use trigram: the subject has the upper hand on the situation.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Dui as body: pleasure, the mouth, exchange, the open surface.
- Zhen as use: sudden movement, initiative from below, the thing that starts things.
- The third line is incorrect in its place and unanswered. Its text: "係丈夫，失小子，隨有求得，利居貞" — one holds to the grown man and loses the boy; in following there is seeking and getting; it is favourable to abide in correctness. An explicit trade: attach to the senior party, give up the junior one.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc is one of realignment. The third line makes a choice between two attachments and takes the more substantial one, accepting the loss of the other as the price. Following here is not passivity — it is an active decision about who to bind oneself to, and it produces what was sought. The mutual hexagram Jian (漸, Gradual Progress) indicates the change is incremental rather than abrupt. The transformation into Ge (革, Revolution) — fire in the lake — is the register shift: what began as following ends as overthrow. Adaptation hardens into replacement.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
A deliberate switch of allegiance that pays off, escalating into something more thoroughgoing than a switch. The flavour is of choosing the stronger horse, getting what one wanted from the choice, and then finding that the whole arrangement is being remade.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- Holding the man and letting go of the boy: a straight trade of a lesser tie for a greater one.
- Thunder inside the lake: energy moving under a pleasant surface.
- Seeking and getting: the rare line text where the wanting and the having are stated together.
- Fire in the marsh: two elements that cannot coexist unchanged — one of them must give.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
The caution 利居貞 sits right after the gain: having got what was sought, the temptation is to keep moving, and the text says stop and hold correct. The third line has no correspondent, so the alignment chosen is not reciprocated from above. The surprise is Ge: Following, the most accommodating hexagram in the set, transforms into the most disruptive.
