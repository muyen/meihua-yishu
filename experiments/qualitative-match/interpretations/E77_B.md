EVENT: NFL Hall of Fame Game, Canton
HEXAGRAM LABEL: B
PRIMARY: 27 山雷頤 -> CHANGED: 24 地雷復 (Line 6)
MUTUAL: 坤為地

1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Mountain above thunder: Yi, the Corners of the Mouth — the open jaws, the hexagram of nourishment. Two firm lines at top and bottom with four yielding lines between them: the shape of a mouth. The judgment: "觀頤，自求口實", observe what nourishes and what a person seeks to fill their own mouth with. The body trigram controls the use trigram: the subject holds the advantage.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Gen above: the mountain, the still upper jaw.
- Zhen below: thunder, the moving lower jaw — in chewing, only the lower jaw moves.
- The top line is incorrect in place but has a correspondent and sits over a yielding line — the orderly firm-over-soft relation. Its text: "由頤，厲吉，利涉大川" — nourishment proceeds from him; danger, but auspicious; favourable to cross the great water. This is the position of the one everyone else is fed by.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc concerns provision — who feeds whom, and on what. The top line is the source: everything below draws sustenance from this position. The pairing 厲吉 is important, and rare: the danger is not removed by the good fortune, they coexist. Carrying responsibility for others' sustenance is hazardous and rewarding at once, and the line then recommends the boldest possible act — crossing the great water. The mutual hexagram is Kun (坤) doubled: pure receptivity through the middle. The transformation into Fu (復, Return) brings everything back to the single line of light returning at the bottom.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
A position of provision held under real risk, rewarded, and resolving into a fresh start. The flavour is of carrying something for others, being exposed by it, coming through, and finding oneself back at a beginning rather than at a summit.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- Open jaws: the upper still, the lower moving — only one side does the work.
- The source everyone feeds from: provision flowing downward from a single position.
- Danger and good fortune in the same breath: neither cancels the other.
- Crossing the great water: the large committed undertaking, recommended at the riskiest moment.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
Yi's standing question is what is being taken in — the hexagram judges nourishment by its quality, not its quantity. The surprise here is the co-presence of 厲 and 吉: the text refuses to resolve the tension. The transformation into Return says the culmination is not an end state; whatever is achieved cycles back to the starting line.
