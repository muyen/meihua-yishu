EVENT: Spider-Man: Brand New Day opening weekend
HEXAGRAM LABEL: B
PRIMARY: 37 風火家人 -> CHANGED: 53 風山漸 (Line 1)
MUTUAL: 火水未濟

1. SITUATION DESCRIPTION
Wind above fire: Jia Ren, The Family. Fire generates wind and the wind carries its influence outward — the hexagram of the well-ordered interior whose order propagates. The judgment favours the correctness of the inner principle. The body trigram drains into the use trigram: the subject expends itself outward.

2. KEY ACTORS / FORCES
- Xun above: wind, the carrying influence.
- Li below: fire at the hearth, the source.
- The first line is correct in place with a proper correspondent, and carries a yielding line above it. Its text: "閑有家，悔亡" — set up boundaries for the household; regret disappears. 閑 is a fence or a bar across a gate: the establishment of limits at the very beginning.

3. PROCESS NARRATIVE
The arc opens with rules laid down before anything has gone wrong. The whole logic of this line is preventive — boundaries are set at the outset, and the consequence is that regret never materialises. The phrase 悔亡 describes the disappearance of a regret that would otherwise have formed. The mutual hexagram Wei Ji (未濟, Before Completion) sits at the centre: everything out of position, the crossing unfinished. The transformation into Jian (漸, Gradual Progress) — the wild goose advancing shore to rock to plateau — converts the household into a staged journey outward, progress that cannot be hurried.

4. QUALITATIVE OUTCOME CHARACTER
Structure established early, preventing trouble rather than fixing it, resolving into slow deliberate advance. The flavour is of a firm and unglamorous beginning whose value is measured by what does not happen, followed by movement that proceeds one stage at a time.

5. SPECIFIC IMAGERY / METAPHORS
- A bar set across the gate: limits established before anyone tests them.
- Fire on the hearth with the wind carrying its warmth outward.
- Regret dissolving before it forms: the value of prevention, which is invisible.
- The wild goose moving shore to rock to plateau: advance in fixed stages.

6. WARNINGS / SURPRISES
There is no warning in this line — it is one of the quietly sound positions in the book, correct in place and properly answered. The mutual hexagram is the discordant note: underneath the ordered household, everything is in the wrong position. The surprise is how much of this reading is about what is avoided rather than what is gained.
